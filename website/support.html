Help with PocketPic on the web, iPhone, iPad, and Mac

FAQ

Common questions

How do I start a daily photo project?

Open the camera, take your first photo, and save it. Each day, the previous photo appears as a ghost overlay so you can match your framing. Your streak updates automatically in the Library.

How do I take a horizontal photo on my phone?

Open Camera. Landscape is the default photo format, even when you hold your phone upright. Tap Landscape to switch to Portrait. Turn your phone sideways for a larger horizontal viewfinder; the shutter stays beside it. The saved photo uses the framing shown in the preview.

Where are my photos saved?

On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, photos are saved to your Apple Photos library in the PocketPic album. The web journal stores a separate library in your browser and syncs it to the configured cloud storage. Import photos to bring your existing collection to the web.

How does the web version sync?

The web journal uploads each photo to a private Cloudflare R2 bucket. Unlock it with the same passphrase on any phone, tablet, or computer and the library syncs when you open it, when you return to the tab, and every 30 seconds while it is open. Changing the passphrase in Settings signs out your other devices.

How do I export a time-lapse on the web?

Open Export in the sidebar or tab bar. Choose a frame rate and whether to align eyes, then select Create Time-lapse. Preview your video and select Download to save it. Your browser chooses the supported video format.

What is eye alignment?

When enabled, PocketPic uses on-device face detection to align each frame so your eyes stay in a consistent position. This makes self-portrait time-lapses look smoother. It runs entirely on your device and can be turned off anytime.

What does Privacy mode do?

Privacy mode replaces photo thumbnails in the in-app gallery with neutral placeholders. Your photos are not deleted — they remain in the Photos app.

How does the Mac menu bar camera work?

PocketPic lives in the menu bar. Click the viewfinder icon to open a compact camera popover for a quick daily shot without launching the full window.

The app can't access my camera or photos

Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera (or Photos) and enable access for PocketPic. You can also open System Settings from the link in PocketPic's Settings screen.

What are the system requirements?

The native app requires iOS 18 or macOS 15 or later. The web journal works in a current browser; camera access needs HTTPS or localhost. Video export support depends on your browser.
